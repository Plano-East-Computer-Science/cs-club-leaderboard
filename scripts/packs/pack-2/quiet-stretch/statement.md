The library's noise meter logs one reading per minute for N minutes. A study group wants the longest stretch of consecutive minutes whose readings add up to at most S. How many minutes long is it?

## Input

The first line has N and S. The second line has the N readings, in order.

## Output

Print the length of the longest stretch of consecutive minutes with a total of at most S. Print 0 if every single reading is more than S.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ N ≤ 50,000
- 0 ≤ each reading ≤ 100
- 0 ≤ S ≤ 1,000,000,000
