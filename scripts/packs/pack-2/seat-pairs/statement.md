The auditorium seating chart has R rows of C seats. A free seat is `.` and a taken seat is `X`. Two friends want two free seats right next to each other in the same row. How many such pairs of seats are there?

Pairs can overlap: a row `...` has two pairs, the first and second seats and the second and third.

## Input

The first line has R and C. Each of the next R lines has C characters, each `.` or `X`.

## Output

Print the number of pairs of free seats that are side by side in the same row.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ R, C ≤ 100
