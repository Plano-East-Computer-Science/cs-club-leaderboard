In the math club's card game, every card has an integer on it, and some are negative. A pair of cards scores if their two numbers add up to exactly K. How many different pairs of cards score?

Two pairs are different if they use a different two cards, even when the numbers are the same.

## Input

The first line has N and K. The second line has N integers: the numbers on the cards.

## Output

Print the number of pairs of positions i < j where card i plus card j equals K.

## Constraints

- 2 ≤ N ≤ 2,000
- -1,000,000 ≤ each card ≤ 1,000,000
- -2,000,000 ≤ K ≤ 2,000,000
