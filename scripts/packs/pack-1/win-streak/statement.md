The tennis team writes its season record as a string: W for a win and L for a loss, in the order the matches were played. What was the team's longest run of wins in a row?

## Input

One line made of the letters W and L.

## Output

Print the length of the longest run of consecutive W. Print 0 if there are no wins at all.

## Constraints

- The line has 1 to 100,000 letters.
- Every letter is W or L.
