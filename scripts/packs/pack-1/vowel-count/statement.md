The yearbook committee is checking photo captions. For one caption, count its vowels.

The vowels are a, e, i, o and u, in lower or upper case. The letter y is not a vowel here.

## Input

One line of text: the caption.

## Output

Print one integer: how many vowels the caption has.

## Constraints

- The line has 1 to 1,000 characters.
- It can contain letters, digits, spaces and punctuation.
