N students are standing in the lunch line. Then M more students arrive, one at a time, and each of them cuts straight to the very front of the line. Print the line after everyone has arrived.

## Input

The first line has N. The second line has the N names in the line, from front to back. The third line has M. The fourth line has the M names of the students who cut, in the order they arrive.

## Output

Print the final line from front to back on one line, with the names separated by single spaces.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ N ≤ 1,000 and 1 ≤ M ≤ 1,000
- Each name is 1 to 12 letters.
- All N + M names are different.
