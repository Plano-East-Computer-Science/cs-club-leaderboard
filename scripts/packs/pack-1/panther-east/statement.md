At the pep rally the crowd counts up from 1. On a multiple of 3 they shout Panther instead of the number, on a multiple of 5 they shout East, and on a multiple of both they shout PantherEast.

## Input

One integer N.

## Output

Print N lines. Line i (for i from 1 to N) is:

- `PantherEast` if i is a multiple of both 3 and 5
- `Panther` if i is a multiple of 3 only
- `East` if i is a multiple of 5 only
- the number i otherwise

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ N ≤ 1,000
